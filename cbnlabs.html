Short-form Video Engine

Generates creative 2D video ideas, expands them into production-ready prompts, sends them to Veo, waits for completion, and records the result in Google Sheets.

1
Scheduled startn8n starts the workflow automatically on its schedule.
2
Idea generationDeepSeek creates a short-form concept with a caption, idea, environment, and status.
3
Prompt productionA second AI agent turns the concept into a detailed video-generation prompt.
4
Generate and trackVeo receives the prompt, n8n waits for the result, then logs the video URL and status in Google Sheets.

LinkedIn Content Engine

Accepts a topic and audience, researches when needed, writes the post, creates a visual prompt and image, uploads the asset, publishes to LinkedIn, and emails the finished result.

1
Collect the briefA form captures the recipient email, post topic, and target audience.
2
Research and writeGemini adapts the post to the topic and can use Tavily for current, credible information.
3
Create the visualAI generates a visual concept, title, and image prompt before creating the LinkedIn graphic.
4
Publish and deliverThe image is converted and uploaded to Drive, the post is published to LinkedIn, and a summary email is sent.
